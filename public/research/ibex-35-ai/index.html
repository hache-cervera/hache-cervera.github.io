Is the IBEX 35 ready for AI search?

I read the robots.txt of Spain’s 35 largest listed companies, then knocked on every front door pretending to be Chrome, Googlebot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot and friends. Same machine, same IP, only the name changes. To tell an AI block from a generic bot block, I invented a control group: PlaceBot, the placebo bot.

/ 01 · The basics

What “ready for AI” means

It is not having an llms.txt. It is classic SEO: a bot can get in, read the page without JavaScript, understand it and find it. Plus a conscious decision about AI bots, which come in two kinds:

KindReads your site toExamplesIf you block it
Traininglearn (train models)GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, BytespiderNot used for training. A legitimate choice.
Searchcite you when someone asksOAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBotYou disappear from those answers.
Useropen a page a person asked forChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-UserThat person cannot read you from the chat. OpenAI notes robots.txt may not apply.

Google-Extended is not a crawler: it is a robots.txt token for Gemini training and does not affect Google Search or AI Overviews, which use regular Googlebot. Block Googlebot or Bingbot and you also leave the AI features built on them.

/ 02 · What they say

/ 04 · The placebo

PlaceBot, the control group

In medicine the control group takes a sugar pill. Here the drug is the bot’s name. PlaceBot is an honest, unknown bot: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; PlaceBot/1.0; +https://www.linkedin.com/in/hache-cervera). Nobody has a rule for it.

If GPTBot is blocked and PlaceBot too, the site blocks any bot. If PlaceBot gets in but a fake Googlebot does not, the site blocks impersonators. Only if both get in is the rule really about that AI. Without the placebo, all three look like “they block AI”.

The twist: Bankinter and Puig block the fake Chrome and let PlaceBot in with full content. Lying about who you are closes more doors than being a nobody.

The rules look at the exact User-Agent: at Colonial, a bare “GPTBot” gets in; the full official string gets 403.

/ 05 · Findings

What stood out

  • Iberdrola, the paradox. The biggest llms.txt in the index (2.4 MB, 7,291 links), and a door that returns 403 to every bot name, placebo included. Without JavaScript its home page has 0 words. Generic anti-bot, not anti-AI, but it says one thing with the file and another with the guard.
  • Colonial does it by the book, silently. Blocks training bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot), lets search bots in (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot). All in Cloudflare; robots.txt says nothing.
  • Ferrovial probably filters “GPT”. GPTBot and ChatGPT-User fall; OAI-SearchBot passes. A hypothesis.
  • Acciona, Acciona Energía, Unicaja. Every famous bot is blocked, the fake Googlebot too, and PlaceBot gets in: anti-impersonation, not anti-AI.
  • Sabadell and Solaria. Sabadell blocks Bingbot and most “…Bot/” strings. Solaria answers 429 to any known bot from the first request.
  • Bankinter’s llms.txt describes bankintercard, its consumer-credit subsidiary, not Bankinter.
  • Indra is the only one blocking a bot in robots.txt: Bytespider (ByteDance). Logista names five AI bots to invite them.

/ 06 · llms.txt

llms.txt is a 2024 proposal

Proposed by Jeremy Howard in September 2024: a short Markdown file at /llms.txt with a title, a summary and key links, meant to fit in a model’s context. Google says you do not need “new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup” to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and John Mueller has said Google does not use it. In a corporate site it is a curiosity, not a score.

/ 07 · Checklist

Is a site ready for AI search?

CheckHow
Get in (sign)robots.txt does not block Googlebot, Bingbot or OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot.
Get in (guard)curl.exe -s -o NUL -w "%{http_code}" -A "<full bot User-Agent>" https://site/, then the same with an invented bot. 403 versus 200 means a rule by name.
ReadDevTools, Disable JavaScript, reload. The content must still be there.
UnderstandClear titles and headings, answers in text, JSON-LD.
FindSitemap, internal links, indexed in Google and Bing.
DecideAllow or block training bots knowingly; it does not change search visibility.
MeasureAI referrals in analytics, bot hits in server logs.

/ 08 · Method

Security layers, and how I handled them

LayerWhat it doesHow it was handled
CDNServes and filters trafficLogged the provider, never read as intent
Bot managementScores every visitCounted words, not only status codes
Verified botsLets real crawlers in by IPThe limit: I test the name, not the IP
Anti-impersonationBlocks fake famous botsThe placebo exposes it
Client fingerprintChecks a “Chrome” behaves like ChromeThe fake Chrome is a baseline, not a user
JS challengeAsks to run JavaScript firstMarked “verif.”, not an AI block
Rate limitSlows fast clientsPauses and random order

Between 15 and 35 requests per site over two days, home page only. The limit: real crawlers come from verified IP ranges and may get a different answer. This shows that some rules look at the bot’s name, not what the real GPTBot sees.
