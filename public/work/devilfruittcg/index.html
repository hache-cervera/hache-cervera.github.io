/ 03 · Strategy

SEO, AEO and GEO: three layers, one piece of content

Most niche sites optimise for Google only. This one is built to rank on three fronts with the same article, without duplicated work.

SEO

Silo architecture and keywords with a filter

  • Audit of four competitors with Screaming Frog before publishing a single line.
  • Every keyword passes three filters: transactional intent, weak Spanish-language competition and an experience advantage of my own. Anything that fails one is dropped, with the reason written down.
  • Audit finding: Reddit ranks top 5 for all six core keywords, which is a direct signal to work the GEO layer.
  • noindex during the build, so the site is not indexed until the content is complete. A deliberate decision, not an oversight.
AEO

Extractable structure for answer engines

  • A "quick answer" block at the top of every article: 40 to 60 self-contained words answering the question in the title, the format ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews actually quote.
  • FAQPage schema generated automatically from the visible HTML, parsing <details><summary> in functions.php. No cloaking, and a single source of truth between what the user sees and what the bot reads.
  • Semantic comparison tables (rarities, set prefixes, card types), the format an LLM extracts best for questions like "what does OP17-046 mean".
  • Deliberate bold density over concrete data points rather than whole sentences, as an extraction signal for snippets.
  • H2s written as natural questions, matching how people actually ask an AI.
GEO

Measuring visibility inside generative engines

  • Monthly tracking protocol: a battery of 20 prompts run against ChatGPT, Perplexity Pro, Google AI and Claude on the first of each month, logged in Google Sheets.
  • Day zero on the record: the "zero mentions" result from before anything was published exists as data. Most projects never have that baseline because they start measuring too late. Here the history starts before the first indexation.
  • Community presence (Reddit, forums, Spanish TCG groups) as a GEO pillar, since those are the same sources the AI cites.
  • EXIF authorship metadata on every original image plus ImageObject schema, so the domain travels with the picture when it gets shared.

/ 04 · Editorial components

Link assets, not just content

To stand apart from the generic affiliate sites in the niche, the site ships interactive components that teach instead of listing. That is the kind of content that earns natural links and that an LLM can cite as an authority on the mechanism, not just on the fact.

Interactive playmat

Nine clickable board zones, each with its own explanation. Content a new player cannot get anywhere else in Spanish, built as a component rather than a paragraph.

Turn phases, step by step

The five phases of a turn as a selectable sequence. Each state is a short, direct answer, which is exactly the shape Google can lift into a featured snippet.

Combat, broken into steps

The part everyone gets wrong, split into ordered steps with the counterplay for each one. Numbered structure reads well for a human and parses cleanly for a model.

Rules FAQ that feeds the schema

Grouped questions built with native <details> elements. The same markup the reader opens is what generates the FAQPage schema, so the two can never drift apart.

Also in production: an annotated card with real hotspots (cost, power, code, rarity), a visual pack simulator with pull rates cited with source and date, and a colour system explaining the six archetypes in one line each.

/ 06 · Editorial rigour

Real experience, verifiable data

Google weighs Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness. Each of the four has a concrete answer in this project.

E-E-A-T signalHow it is implemented
Experience My own collection, tracked in an app. Photos of real cards I own, no stock and no official Bandai art. First-hand data: pull rates and real purchase prices.
Expertise Pull rates and tournament meta figures cited with source and date. An explicit note whenever two sources disagree, editorial honesty instead of inventing consensus. No invented data left unmarked.
Authoritativeness Interactive components that demonstrate deep knowledge of the object itself: the card, the playmat, the mechanics. Linkable assets that earn natural references.
Trustworthiness Affiliate disclosure in the footer from day one. rel="sponsored nofollow" on every commercial link. Prices flagged as approximate. Explicit placeholders wherever a verified figure is still missing.
